nKABE OS: Pilot
expression of interest.

Nkabe is inviting organisations and technology partners to express interest in exploring a potential real-world pilot of nKABE OS.

Nkabe welcomes expressions of interest from businesses, schools, colleges, institutions and technology partners that may have an environment where nKABE OS could be explored and evaluated.

We are currently working through prototype development, hardware validation and pilot preparation. We welcome conversations with organisations that have existing computers, practical computing needs or an environment in which the product could be evaluated.

Organisations interested in discussing a potential pilot are invited to share a little about their environment, the computers available and the needs they would like to explore.

Submitting an expression of interest does not guarantee selection or confirm a pilot. Submissions will be reviewed and followed up where there is a suitable opportunity to explore further.
