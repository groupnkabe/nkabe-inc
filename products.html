FLAGSHIP VENTURE

nKABE OS™

Intelligent infrastructure for the computers you already own.

nKABE OS explores a new approach to computing infrastructure, helping unlock greater capability from existing hardware.

An offline-first operating system in development to help turn existing desktops and laptops into useful local infrastructure. The product is being developed around understanding a machine, matching it to a practical role, and preparing a suitable configuration.

01

Assess the hardware
Identify a computer’s capabilities and constraints before deployment.

02

Deploy with purpose
Prepare a machine for a defined role, such as local storage or services.

03

Operate with visibility
Explore bringing key local system functions into a unified management experience.
