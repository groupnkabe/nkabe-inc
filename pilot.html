We are preparing real-world validation for nKABE OS. Tell us about your organisation, the computers you already have and the practical needs you would like to explore.

What happens after you express interest?

We review each submission to understand the organisation, its computing environment and the use cases it wants to explore. Where there may be a fit, we follow up to discuss requirements and arrange a more detailed technical assessment.

Sharing your information does not guarantee selection or confirm a pilot. Any assessment or deployment would be discussed and agreed separately.
