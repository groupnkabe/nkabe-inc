PRODUCT UPDATE19 November 2026

nKABE OS: Pilot expression of interest

Nkabe is inviting businesses, schools, colleges, institutions and technology partners to express interest in exploring a potential real-world pilot of nKABE OS.

We are currently working through prototype development, hardware validation and pilot preparation. We welcome conversations with organisations that have existing computers, practical computing needs or an environment in which the product could be evaluated.

Submitting an expression of interest does not guarantee selection or confirm a pilot. Submissions will be reviewed and followed up where there is a suitable opportunity to explore further.
